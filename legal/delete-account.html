Delete Your Account & Data

Last updated: 11 July 2026

This page explains how to delete your Kay's Market account and the personal data linked to it, what is removed, and what we may be required to keep. It applies to the Kay's Market mobile app.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest)

You can permanently delete your account yourself, at any time:

  1. Open the Kay's Market app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and data are then permanently removed.
Wallet balance: if you still have money in your Kay's Market wallet, you'll be asked to withdraw it to your bank first — this protects you from losing funds. Once your wallet is empty, deletion can proceed.

Option 2 — Request deletion without the app

If you can't access the app, email support@kaysmarket.com.ng from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We may ask a question or two to confirm it's really you before we proceed. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your profile — name, phone number, email, password and profile photo.
  • Your identity/KYC data, including your NIN.
  • Your chat messages, images and videos.
  • Your saved bank account details and wallet.
  • Your cart, and (for vendors) your store and product listings.
  • Your sign-in / login credentials.

What we may keep, and why

For legal and regulatory reasons, we may retain a limited set of financial and transaction records (for example, records of completed orders and payments) for the period required by Nigerian tax, accounting and anti-fraud laws. These are kept securely, used only for those purposes, and deleted or anonymised once no longer required.

Please note: deletion cannot be undone, and any open orders, pending deliveries or unresolved disputes should be completed before you delete your account.

Questions?

Contact us at support@kaysmarket.com.ng. For more on how we handle your data, see our Privacy Policy.
