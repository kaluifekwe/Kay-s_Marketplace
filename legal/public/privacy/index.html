Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 July 2026  •  Effective: 11 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kay's Market ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal information when you use the Kay's Market mobile application and related services (the "App"). Kay's Market is a Nigeria-based online marketplace that connects buyers and vendors, with escrow-protected orders, in-app chat, a wallet, and delivery.

We are committed to handling your data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023 (NDPA) and the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR). By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

1. Who we are

The App is operated by Kay's Market. For any privacy question or request, contact us at support@kaysmarket.com.ng.

2. Information we collect

We only collect what we need to run the marketplace safely. This includes:

CategoryExamplesWhy we collect it
Account details Full name, phone number, email address, password, account type (buyer or vendor), profile photo. To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
Identity / KYC Your National Identification Number (NIN) and the identity details returned during verification (e.g. your legal name). To verify you are a real person before you buy or sell, prevent fraud, and meet regulatory obligations. Verification is performed by our provider, Prembly (IdentityPass).
Vendor / store data Store name, product listings, prices, descriptions, product images. To display your store and products to buyers (if you are a vendor).
Payment & financial Bank account details (for withdrawals/refunds), virtual account number, wallet balance, transaction history. To process payments, wallet funding, escrow, refunds and payouts. Card and bank payment processing is handled by our payment partners — we do not store your full card number.
Delivery information Pickup and delivery addresses, recipient name and phone number, order contents. To arrange and track delivery of your orders through our delivery partners.
Communications Messages, images and videos you send in the in-app chat; delivery-fee requests; disputes and support messages. To let buyers and vendors communicate, and to help resolve disputes.
Device & usage Push-notification token, "last active" status, app version, device platform, and diagnostic/error logs. To deliver notifications, keep the App reliable, and diagnose problems.

3. Device permissions

The App asks for these permissions on your device, only for the stated purpose. You can decline or revoke them in your device settings.

  • Camera — to take photos for chats or product listings.
  • Photos & media — to attach images or videos you choose from your device.
  • Notifications — to alert you about orders, payments, delivery and chat messages.
  • Internet — required for the App to function.

4. How we use your information

  • Create, secure and manage your account.
  • Verify your identity and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • Process orders, payments, escrow, wallet funding, refunds and payouts.
  • Arrange and track deliveries.
  • Enable chat between buyers and vendors and resolve disputes.
  • Send you service messages and notifications about your activity.
  • Maintain, improve and troubleshoot the App.
  • Comply with legal, regulatory and tax obligations.

5. Legal bases for processing

Under the NDPA, we rely on: your consent; performance of a contract with you (providing the marketplace); our legitimate interests (fraud prevention, security, improving the service); and compliance with a legal obligation.

7. Data retention

We keep your personal data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account (see Section 9), we delete your profile, identity, chat and related data. Certain records — particularly financial and transaction records — may be retained for a longer period where we are legally required to keep them (for example for tax, accounting, anti-fraud or regulatory reasons), after which they are deleted or anonymised.

8. How we protect your data

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS), access controls, and escrow to protect funds during a transaction. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information and to notify you and the authorities of any breach where required by law.

9. Your rights & deleting your account

Under the NDPA you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time.

You can delete your account and personal data directly in the App: go to Settings → Delete Account. (If you have money in your wallet, you'll be asked to withdraw it to your bank first.) You can also request deletion or exercise any other right via our Account & Data Deletion page or by emailing support@kaysmarket.com.ng.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

10. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18. Because we require NIN verification and handle payments, we do not knowingly allow minors to create accounts. If you believe a minor has used the App, contact us and we will remove the account.

11. International transfers

Some of our service providers may process data outside Nigeria. Where this happens, we take steps to ensure your data receives an adequate level of protection consistent with the NDPA.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here and change the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes may also be notified in the App.

13. Contact us

Questions or requests about your privacy? Email support@kaysmarket.com.ng.
